Hey Palak, my SweetHeart 💖, my Cappuccino ☕, my Malkin Ji 👑... ♡

🎉 Happy Birthday, my love! 🎉 🎂 ✨

I don't even know where to start, because there's so much I want to say and so many little things I love about you. I could try to make this the most perfect letter ever, but honestly, I just want it to sound like me talking to you — because that's what you deserve.

We've been through a lot of days together. Like, literal each-and-every-day kinds of days. Some must have been easy, some probably not so easy, and some where all we could do was ask each other, "Aps ho payega?" 😂 And somehow, that tiny question says so much about us. About trying. About figuring things out. About being a little silly even when life gets serious. About looking at each other and choosing to keep going, one day at a time. 🫂

And then there's you. The way you smile — God, your smile. ☺️ It can make my day the best without even trying. The way you breathe, the way you exist, the way you can just be there and make an ordinary moment feel important. I love those little details that might seem small to someone else. To me, they're you. And you are never a small thing to me. ❤️

But there's something I think about, too: what makes your day the best? I hope it's not only me saying all these things. I hope life is gentle with you. I hope you get reasons to smile that belong only to you — dreams coming true, good surprises, peace in your mind, people appreciating you, moments where you feel proud of yourself, and days when you look around and think, "Yaar, life is actually beautiful." 🌸 You deserve that kind of happiness, not just today but on regular random Tuesdays too.

I love that you're my SweetHeart. I love calling you my Cappuccino ☕. And calling you my Malkin Ji? Obviously, madam, that is a position of great authority 🧐 and I accept my duties — including but not limited to listening to you, making you smile, planning cozy dates, and being available for cuddles when the world gets too much. 🎀

I want more café dates with you ☕ 🍰. More sitting across from you and forgetting to check the time. More walks where we stop because a cat decided we're friends now 🐱, or a street dog deserves a little kindness🐶. More conversations that begin with something random and somehow end up being about everything. More laughter, more food stealing 🍟, more pictures, more memories, and more of those "just us" moments where nobody needs to be impressive or have everything figured out.

And if some days aren't perfect, I don't want us to feel like we have to pretend they are. I want us to be able to talk, listen, learn, apologise when needed, laugh when we can, and give each other room to be human. No grand promises that life will always be easy — just a wish to keep meeting the days honestly, with care for each other. 🤝 ❤️

On your birthday, I hope you feel celebrated for the person you already are. Not for what you do for everyone else, not for how much you give, not for being strong all the time. Just because you're Palak. Because you are you. And that is already worth celebrating in a thousand different ways. 🎂

Thank you for being part of my days, for all the moments we've collected, for the smiles, the silliness, the questions, and the little world that's ours. I feel lucky that I get to know you this closely and keep discovering new things to love about you. 🥺 💖

So here's to another year of you being wonderfully you. I hope this year brings you softness when you're tired, courage when you're unsure, joy when you least expect it, and a whole lot of reasons to smile that make your cheeks hurt. 😊 🌟

And here's to us making more memories — slowly, sweetly, in our own way. No rush. Just us. ♡

Happy Birthday, Palak. I love you. Always, in all the little ways and the big ones. 💕

Yours, Akash always ♡
